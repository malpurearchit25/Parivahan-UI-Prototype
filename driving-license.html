A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you get going.

Clear expectations make a better journey.

Is this an official government website?

No. This is an independent student project exploring the design of transport services. It is not affiliated with Parivahan or any government authority.

Can I apply or make a payment here?

No applications, payments, or document uploads take place here. Service cards provide illustrative guides and a link to the official portal.

Does the lookup access real records?

No. It reads two fictional records bundled with the page. Use the sample buttons; do not enter personal identifiers.
